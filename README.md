Key Insights
Strong profitability: $29.36M in sales at a 41.1% profit margin.
2013 was the peak year: $16.4M in sales vs $7.1M (2011) and $5.8M (2012). Sales dipped in 2012 before the 2013 jump.
Q4 is the strongest quarter, contributing 31.1% of profit; Q1 is the weakest at 18.7%.
Bikes drive revenue, accessories drive volume: Road Bikes ($14.5M), Mountain Bikes ($10.0M) and Touring Bikes ($3.8M) generate most sales, while Accessories account for about 60% of units sold.
Mountain Bikes have the best bike margin (45.4%), vs 38.1% for Road Bikes and 37.8% for Touring Bikes.
Customer base is concentrated in a few occupations: Professionals generate the most sales ($9.9M), Manual workers the least ($2.9M).
United States ($9.39M) and Australia ($9.06M) lead on sales; together they make up over 60% of revenue. Profit margin is steady at about 41% across all countries, with Canada highest at 42.0%.
Recommendations
Prioritise Mountain Bikes: highest-margin bike line, so promotion there adds the most profit.
Bundle accessories with bike purchases to lift order value, since they sell in high volume.
Run retention campaigns to raise the 37% repeat-buyer rate, starting with top customers.
Plan inventory and marketing around Q4, the peak profit quarter.
Grow UK, Germany, France and Canada, which together contribute far less than the US and Australia despite similar margins.
